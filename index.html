How do I get the 5% off on my first order?

Just place your first order through any WhatsApp button on this page. The 5% discount is applied automatically when we confirm your bill — no coupon code needed.

What does same-day dispatch mean?

Orders confirmed before 4 PM leave our store the same working day in protective packaging. You'll receive tracking details on WhatsApp as soon as your parcel ships.

How long does pan-India delivery take?

Metro cities usually receive orders in 2–4 working days; other towns take 4–7 working days depending on the courier route. We share the exact estimate before you pay.

Are the books 100% original?

Yes. Every title is sourced from publishers or authorised distributors. If you ever receive a misprint or damaged copy, we replace it free of charge.

Can I order in bulk for a school, library or office?

Absolutely. Send your full list on WhatsApp for a bulk quote — schools, libraries and reading clubs get special pricing on 20+ copies.
